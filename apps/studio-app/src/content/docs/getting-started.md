# Getting started

Kukui Studio runs entirely in your browser. There's no account to create, no software to install, and no server we operate that needs to be up. You'll be authoring your first activity in about five minutes.

## What you'll do

1. Open Studio
2. Pick an activity type
3. Fill in the form (or describe what you want in plain English)
4. Preview live
5. Download a SCORM zip and upload it to your LMS

## Step 1 — Open Studio

Go to **[kukuistudio.com/studio](/studio)** in any modern browser. You'll land in the editor with a sidebar of activity types on the left and a live preview on the right.

Your work is saved to your browser's local storage automatically. Closing the tab won't lose your draft; coming back to the same browser will pick up where you left off.

## Step 2 — Pick an activity type

The left sidebar lists 24 activity types, organized by **Bloom's revised taxonomy**:

- **Remember** — recall facts (Flashcards, Matching Pairs, Crossword)
- **Understand** — identify, explain, classify (Hotspots, Anatomy Labeling, Highlight Text)
- **Apply** — use procedures in new contexts (Drag and Drop, Sequence Steps, Virtual Tour)
- **Analyze** — break apart, compare (Image Annotation, Concept Map, Lab Panel)
- **Evaluate** — judge, critique, decide (Branching Scenario, OSCE, Reflection)
- **Create** — produce original work (Audio Recording)

Pick the level that matches what you want learners to do. Click the activity name to load its editor.

> **Tip:** If you're not sure, **Flashcards** is the easiest activity to start with — just front/back text, no media required.

## Step 3 — Fill in the form

The editor has three tabs across the top:

- **Editor** — the form-driven view (recommended for first-time authors)
- **Raw JSON** — direct JSON editing, for power users and version-controlled workflows
- **AI Assist** — describe what you want in plain English and let AI draft the activity

Every activity has at minimum a **title** and a **prompt** (the instruction shown to learners). Beyond that, fields vary by activity type — Flashcards has cards, Drag and Drop has draggable chips and target zones, and so on.

Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (`*`). The status badge at the top of the editor shows **Valid** or the number of remaining validation issues.

## Step 4 — Preview live

The right pane shows the activity as a learner will see it. As you edit the form, the preview updates immediately — no save button needed.

The preview has two modes for some activities:

- **Edit** — drag elements directly on a canvas (Drag and Drop, Hotspots)
- **Live** — interact with the activity as a learner would

## Step 5 — Download a SCORM zip

Once your activity reads **Valid**, click the **Download SCORM 1.2 ZIP** button in the top right. Studio packages your activity into a `.zip` file named after your title.

Drop that zip into your LMS. See [Upload to your LMS](/docs/upload-to-lms) for the steps in Brightspace, Canvas, and Moodle.
